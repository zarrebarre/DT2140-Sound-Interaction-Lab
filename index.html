    • Wind sailing sound

    Hold the phone flat in your hand and tilt it side-to-side, you will hear a wind noise that changes in intensity depending on the tilt of the phone. The more you tilt, the stronger the wind sound will be. This interaction uses the device's accelerometer triugh the function rotationChange to detect the tilt and modulate the sound accordingly. We imagined a boat that is sailing on the sea, the user is controlling the boats angle and so the wind sound naturally follows this. It also makes sense because depending on how much you tilt the phone, the wind will be stronger or weaker.
